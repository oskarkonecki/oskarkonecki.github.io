GroWirus

In this game, you take control of a deadly virus with one goal - escape the lab and take over the world! But it's not that simple. You've infiltrated a scientist's brain, but you need to grow stronger before you can leave the room. The best way to achieve your goal?

Blend in!

Complete the tasks given to the scientist to maintain your cover, and watch how your virus spreads inside their body. But be careful - make one or two suspicious mistakes, and your existence will be discovered

About the Project

This game was created for Untitled Game Jam #123. This is my very first Game Jam, so I really hope you will enjoy it! All 3D assets and scripts were made by me during the jam.
